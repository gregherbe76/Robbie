Build the Public Cognition Lab for the Recruiting Intelligence Framework.

The framework already has:
- public landing
- deterministic replay demo
- benchmarks
- provenance
- calibration
- cognition engine
- organization intelligence
- collaborative operations
- security boundaries

- an ATS

--------------------------------------------------

- uploading resumes into AI.

- replay-centric

- uncertainty-visible
